Format your invoice sheet for GST Recon

Upload your own spreadsheet, match its columns once, review GSTIN and invoice checks, then download a clean site-ready file. Excel numbers and dates are normalized to the format the reconciliation tools accept.

This prepares data for GST Recon's reconciliation importer; it does not create tax invoices or file GST returns.

🔒

Private by design. This formatter reads the selected file in this browser tab only. It does not upload the spreadsheet, save it to an account, or send its contents to an API. Your source file is never changed; formatting creates a separately named download. Closing the tab or choosing “Clear file” removes the working preview.

1 · Choose a spreadsheet

Accepted formats: Excel (.xlsx / .xls), CSV, TSV, text or JSON. Maximum 10 MB. If an Excel workbook has several sheets, choose the one with invoice rows.

Only the selected sheet is formatted.

Site-ready columns: Supplier GSTIN, Invoice No, Invoice Date, Taxable Value, CGST, SGST, IGST, Narration. GSTIN and Invoice No are required by the site's current importer; missing tax amounts export as 0, and missing date/narration stay blank. For sales files, put the buyer/customer GSTIN in the GSTIN column; the target site treats this as the counterparty GSTIN.

3 · Match your columns

We auto-match common GST, Tally and portal headings. Check every match; you can change any dropdown. Required columns are marked *.

4 · Review and download

GSTIN checks use the same format/check-digit rules as the site; they do not confirm registration status. Invalid or incomplete rows remain in the export with a review warning, so check them before reconciling.
