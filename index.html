How matching works

A row counts as matched only when supplier GSTIN, invoice number, invoice date, taxable value, CGST, SGST and IGST are all exactly equal in both files. Everything else lands in one of these buckets:

Missing in 2B

In your Purchase Register but not in GSTR-2B — ITC you may not be able to claim yet.

Extra in 2B

In GSTR-2B but not in your register — purchases you may have missed booking.

Value & tax mismatches

Same GSTIN and invoice number in both files, but the taxable value or the CGST / SGST / IGST amounts differ.

Duplicates & invalid GSTINs

Same invoice entered twice in one file, and GSTINs that fail the official check-digit validation — each with the exact reason.
