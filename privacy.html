Privacy Policy — GST Recon

Last updated: 27 November 2026

GST Recon ("the Service") is a free GST reconciliation tool for Indian businesses and accountants. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

1. Your data stays in your browser

All reconciliation processing happens in your own browser. Your purchase-register and GSTR-2B files are read locally on your device — they are not uploaded to any server. We do not see, store or sell your business data.

2. Accounts (optional)

You may sign in with Google or email (via Firebase Authentication) to use account features. We receive only the basic profile information needed to identify your account (such as your name and email address). Your password, if any, is handled by Firebase and is never visible to us.

3. API keys you enter

If you enter a third-party API key (for example, for GSTIN lookup), it is saved only in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to our servers. It is sent directly from your browser to that third-party service when you use the lookup feature.

4. Advertising — Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

5. Analytics and cookies

We may use cookies or local storage to remember your preferences (such as theme or login state). No advertising or tracking cookies are set by us directly; any such cookies come from Google AdSense as described above.

6. Third-party links

The Service may link to third-party websites (for example, government GST portals). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for businesses and professionals. It is not directed at children under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the site owner through the GST Recon website.
